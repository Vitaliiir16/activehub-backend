## Demonstration for the teacher

1. Start the two sibling checkouts using the backend README. Enable SEED_DEMO with a private DEMO_PASSWORD and namespace. Show API readiness and Mailpit; explain separate API/frontend/worker and PostgreSQL volumes.
2. Sign in as member1. Browse photos, kinds, city/direction and availability filters; open an activity and view its organizer and plans. Open My subscriptions, Loyalty, booking calendar, Messages and Notifications.
3. Sign in as the trainer in another browser session. Show real dashboard counters, requests, upcoming capacity and activity cards. Create a future activity with a positive price and AUTO confirmation.
4. As the member, find that activity, inspect the server price breakdown, reserve and pay with test card 4242 4242 4242 4242, any holder/future expiry and a test CVV. Show the confirmed receipt and points. Cancel at least 12 hours before its start; show full refund, restored availability and original-award reversal. No real money moves.
5. Buy/use a ONE_TIME or MONTHLY plan, show visit consumption and cancellation restoration. Use member2 for a locked Premium/free-direction example and member3 for the corporate subscription/company/card example. Explain that exhausted corporate visits do not end card eligibility before calendar expiry.
6. As the activity owner, approve or reject a pending request and show message/read state. Show one in-app event and its captured Mailpit email. Explain optional email preferences versus mandatory booking email.
7. Sign in as the configured administrator. Show user/organizer/event searches, audited moderation, dictionaries, loyalty configuration and period statistics. Organizer revenue includes its subscription sales. Create another administrator through the mandatory form; demonstrate self/last-active protections without leaving the demo locked out.
8. Show the Use Case/class/sequence SVGs and actual pattern symbols from the README. Restart the stack with the same volumes, then show the retained records. Repeating seed preserves edits; a fresh namespace gives fresh dates without deleting prior data.
